# Modeling sources

## Temple University

- Temple University Office of Sustainability / FY2025 Sustainability Action
  Plan: campus gross square footage and campus electricity calibration anchors.
- Temple University iGEM / public SERC documentation: SERC provides
  approximately 250,000 ft² for education and interdisciplinary research.
- Temple Campus Operations: official addresses for Beury Hall and the College
  of Engineering & Architecture.
- Temple College of Engineering directory: current office locations include
  Engineering room 907, used only to support the estimated floor-count
  assumption. It is not an authoritative gross-area source.

## DOE / NREL ComStock

- ComStock data documentation:
  https://natlabrockies.github.io/ComStock.github.io/docs/data.html
- End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock, OEDI/NREL.
- NREL's documentation states ComStock building outputs are available at
  15-minute resolution and separated by end-use categories.

The repository does not label any compact fixture as a raw ComStock export.
