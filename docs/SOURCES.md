# Modeling sources

## Temple University

- Temple University public ArcGIS building services: current building identities, footprints, locations, and authoritative gross/floor-area attributes used by the campus sync.
- Temple University Office of Sustainability / FY2025 sustainability reporting: campus gross square footage and electricity calibration anchor.

## City of Philadelphia

- 2024 Building Energy Benchmarking disclosure: building-level annual electricity and floor area when an unambiguous Temple property match is available.

## DOE / NREL ComStock

- ComStock data documentation:
  https://natlabrockies.github.io/ComStock.github.io/docs/data.html
- End-Use Load Profiles for the U.S. Building Stock, OEDI/NREL.
- Temple Twin uses Philadelphia County 15-minute ComStock end-use profiles.

## Weather

- Open-Meteo historical archive API supplies the current interactive Friday's hourly weather and irradiance.

## Carbon

- EPA eGRID2023 RFC East (RFCE) total-output CO2e emission rate is used for grid-electricity carbon estimates.
