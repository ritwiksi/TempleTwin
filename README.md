Explore how Temple University buildings consume electricity every 15 minutes, visualize their carbon impact in an interactive 3D campus, and simulate sustainability upgrades including rooftop solar, LED retrofits, and HVAC efficiency improvements.

Built using Temple GIS data, Philadelphia energy benchmarking, NREL ComStock, historical weather, EPA eGRID, Cesium, React, FastAPI, Tiger Data, and Snowflake Cortex.
